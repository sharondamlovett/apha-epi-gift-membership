How it works

01

Members sign up

Members submit their full name, email address, and a short bio through the Google Form.

02

Profiles are reviewed

Submissions are collected privately in Google Forms and its linked Google Sheet. An administrator reviews each submission before adding an approved profile to the directory.

03

Sponsors connect

Potential sponsors browse approved profiles and contact members using the email address they chose to make public.
