How it works

01

Members sign up

Members submit their contact details and a short bio explaining their interests and why an APHA membership waiver would be meaningful.

02

Profiles are reviewed

Submissions are not published automatically. A directory administrator reviews and approves each profile.

03

Sponsors connect

Potential sponsors browse approved profiles and contact members using the email address they chose to make public.
