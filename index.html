Gift Membership Database

Searchable database of dedicated current APHA members seeking financial support for a 1-year APHA membership waiver. Prospective donors can review the profiles of prospective recipients, identify which recipient(s) they wish to gift to, and contact APHA staff directly at 1-877-396-APHA (2742) weekdays between 9–5pm Eastern Time to coordinate payment. Please do not attempt to coordinate payment with prospective recipients. All donors and recipients will receive an email receipt of the transaction/processing of the APHA membership from APHA staff.
